# doubt-vs-grove-review-mechanics-k1

**Kind:** requirements

## Goal

Grill the human to establish the exact boundary between in-session
doubt-driven review and Grove-managed review work.

## Context

The observed failure was another Grove work item performing many internal
doubt-driven reviews where fresh Grove sessions, routed to a different model and
harness, were expected to own the adversarial review.

## Done when

- The boundary is confirmed across producers, review/integration leaves,
  research pairs, recovery from a plain producer, and sessions outside Grove.
- Review-budget and routing-diversity expectations are falsifiable.
- Terms are sharpened inline in `CONTEXT.md`.
- The outcome is composed into the root brief and downstream load-bearing work
  is cut as reviewed chains.
