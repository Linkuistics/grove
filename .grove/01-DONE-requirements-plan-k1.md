# plan-k1

## Goal

Establish what this grove is for, record it as the root charter, and cut the work
into reviewable leaves.

## Context

The bootstrap leaf of a fresh grove — the requirements / design / planning fusion
a workstream is allowed at its start. Its only input is the human's own words.

The requirements arrived already settled: an audit of the installed prompt corpus
was complete before this grove began, and its conclusions were handed over as
decisions rather than as questions. So no grilling was staged — a pre-decided
question is not a grilling question, and interrogating settled ground costs a
human's attention and returns nothing.

## Done when

- The root `BRIEF.md` carries the goal, the done-criteria, the constraints, and
  the decomposition rationale.
- `CONTEXT.md` carries the terms the requirements settled.
- The work is cut into ordered leaves, each with a body specific enough that a
  fresh session can execute it from the brief chain alone.

## Notes

- Nine children, ordered by dependency: design first because it decides where
  every rule lives, behavioural coverage second because it must be green before
  the rewrites start and after they land.
- No `review-*` steps were pre-cut. The chain is lazy — each producer decides at
  the end of its own session whether review is warranted, and writes the specific
  doubt into the leaf it cuts. The leaves most likely to need one say so in their
  own Notes.
