# walkthrough-k2

## Goal

Turn the approved book and reusable-skill agreement into a small, ordered Grove
tree whose leaves can be completed and verified independently.

## Context

- Treat `plan-k1` and the root brief as settled requirements; synthesise them
  without re-interviewing the human.
- Inventory the exact production library, manifest, and demonstration-CLI source
  that the fragment graph must reconstruct.
- The work has two coupled deliverables: the `ordinal-fs-tree` book and the
  generic Linkuistics `writing-code-walkthroughs` skill.

## Done when

- The tree orders communication-method research, book/fragment-system design,
  deterministic validation tooling, book authoring, technical review, editorial
  review, skill baseline evaluation, skill authoring, and skill-enabled
  evaluation at their real dependency seams.
- Each leaf fits one focused session and delivers a verifiable vertical slice or
  uses an explicitly justified research/review composition shape.
- Node briefs carry the self-contained-book contract, fragment tangling seam,
  prose contract, and skill-authoring test discipline to their descendants.
- The plan names where final source coverage, Markdown/link checks, crate
  verification, technical accuracy, editorial quality, and skill behavior are
  proven.

## Notes

External research is about exposition and learning design, including the balance
between local repetition and cross-references. Technical research is not a
separate workstream: source, tests, models, and existing crate documentation are
the evidence to inspect while writing and reviewing.
