**Kind:** work

## Goal

Deep-dive **obra/superpowers** (https://github.com/obra/superpowers) as a survey
source. It ships the entire **workflow/process skill class** we lack plus a
`writing-skills` authoring meta-skill. End with a **takeaway-for-skills** and a
**takeaway-for-grove**.

  we already get for free by depending on it?"** — not "discover it."

  for the target split.
