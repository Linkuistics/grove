# walkthrough-method-k4

## Goal

Produce a second, independent survey of methods for teaching a substantial
codebase through concept-first prose and exact literate source fragments.

## Context

- The downstream consumers are `book-system-k6` and
  `walkthrough-skill-delivery-k19`.
- Work from an independent corpus and framing. Do not read or reuse the output
  of `walkthrough-method-k3` before this report is complete.
- External research is limited to exposition and learning design; technical
  truth for `ordinal-fs-tree` comes from this repository and authoritative
  dependency documentation when necessary.

## Done when

- The report evaluates approaches to sequencing concepts, explaining complete
  operations, choosing worked examples, and exposing detail progressively to a
  Rust- and OS-proficient audience.
- It provides evidence for balancing local repetition against internal links
  according to the logical relationship between passages.
- It investigates literate-programming and code-walkthrough techniques that
  permit explanatory reordering while retaining byte-exact source coverage.
- It names common editorial and navigational defects in multi-page technical
  books and how to detect them.
- Claims carry primary or authoritative citations, and inferences are labelled.
- The closing synthesis answers the concrete design and reusable-skill
  questions rather than offering a broad literature catalogue.

## Notes

Bias the corpus away from the most obvious introductory sources so the paired
survey buys genuinely different evidence and blind spots.
