# walkthrough-method-k5

## Goal

Combine the two independent communication-method surveys into one reconciled,
cited evidence base for the book-system design and generic skill.

## Context

- Inputs: `walkthrough-method-k3` and `walkthrough-method-k4`.
- The settled prose and cross-reference contracts in `plan-k1` and the root
  brief are constraints, not questions to reopen.

## Done when

- Agreements, disagreements, corpus gaps, and confidence levels are explicit.
- The synthesis recommends a concept-ordering method, worked-example strategy,
  progressive-disclosure scheme, and navigation pattern suitable for this book.
- It gives a decision rule for local repetition versus cross-reference that
  preserves self-contained explanations without redundant source splitting.
- It states concrete requirements for the fragment system and its explanation
  to readers.
- It distils candidate generic instructions and observable behaviours for the
  later baseline and skill-enabled scenarios.
- No recommendation depends on an uncited material external claim.

## Notes

Preserve meaningful disagreement instead of averaging it away. The book-system
design can choose deliberately only when the trade-off remains visible.
