# transfer-adjudication-k70

## Goal

Audit, blindly score, and publish the separately frozen transfer verdict.

## Context

- Assignment records: `transfer-runs-k69`.
- Frozen transfer criteria, auditor, scorer instruments, thresholds, and
  unavailable semantics: `campaign-freeze-k59`.

## Done when

- Every access, exposure, outcome, and fixture mutation check is classified
  deterministically before scoring.
- Two independent blind contexts score randomized normalized bundles and the
  frozen blind resolver records all disagreement citations.
- The report publishes every atomic and pair-aware count, absolute attainment,
  material delta, regression, residual unblinding, and distinct
  delivery/read/announcement/adherence observation.
- The result is labelled complete, protocol-failed, or unavailable exactly as
  frozen and makes no same-case, population, reader-outcome, or wording-causation
  claim.

## Notes

The transfer result remains visible even when adverse; it cannot be pooled with
the larger same-case row set or used to change treatment bytes.
