# scope-runs-k61

## Goal

Execute the frozen paired scope-elicitation assignments without selecting on
skill discovery or substantive outcome.

## Context

- Joint freeze: `campaign-freeze-k59`.
- Use only the frozen runner, templates, prompt, schedule, access grammar, and
  deployed-skill manifest.

## Done when

- Every one of the five control/enabled pairs reaches its assigned terminal
  record, with replacement eligibility computed from the recorded exposure
  phase and proven pre-exposure failures charged only to the frozen resource
  budget.
- Reading the active skill directory through the allowed grammar is valid in
  both arms; post-exposure access violations, refusals, timeouts, missing finals,
  and non-discovery remain assigned outcomes.
- Raw streams, complete manifests, prompt bytes, runtime identity, pair/order,
  back-to-back per-arm start/end timestamps, complete attempt history, and
  preliminary delivery/read/announcement observations are preserved under the
  new recovery-cycle namespace.
- No behavior score, rubric edit, treatment edit, or historical rescore occurs.

## Notes

Between pair-atomic execution windows, automatic resumption continues at the
frozen schedule's earliest incomplete pair without inspecting arm or outcome.
Mid-pair reserve exhaustion makes the pair terminally unavailable rather than
resuming its second arm later. A terminal shortfall is published rather than
repaired selectively with a later answer.
