# exposition-adjudication-k66

## Goal

Audit, blindly score, and publish the frozen exposition/assurance case result.

## Context

- Assignment records: `exposition-runs-k65`.
- Frozen criteria, auditor, scorer instruments, and outcome rules:
  `campaign-freeze-k59`.

## Done when

- Deterministic access and exposure classification replays replacement legality
  over the complete attempt history before any behavioral bundle is scored. An
  illegal replacement marks the case protocol-failed and excludes it from
  attainment.
- Two independent blind scorers apply the frozen atomic rules to every scored
  randomized normalized bundle and record forced arm guesses; frozen blind
  resolution records every disagreement and citation.
- The case record reports local completeness, repetition/link decisions, prose,
  proposed mechanical assurance, independent review planning, absolute
  attainment, material deltas, regressions, pair-aware counts, arm-guess
  accuracy as a limitation, and separate treatment-delivery observations.
- A complete, protocol-failed, or unavailable verdict is published without
  refining the treatment; missing, unavailable, protocol-failed, and unblindable
  data never contribute to attainment.

## Notes

Judged plans to run deterministic checks remain answer behavior. They are not
reported as proof that a future walkthrough would pass those checks.
