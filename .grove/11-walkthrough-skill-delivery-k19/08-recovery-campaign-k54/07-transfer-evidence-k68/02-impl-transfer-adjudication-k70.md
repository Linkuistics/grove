# transfer-adjudication-k70

## Goal

Audit, blindly score, and publish the separately frozen transfer verdict.

## Context

- Assignment records: `transfer-runs-k69`.
- Frozen transfer criteria, auditor, scorer instruments, thresholds, and
  unavailable semantics: `campaign-freeze-k59`.

## Done when

- Complete attempt history is replayed before scoring; every replacement,
  access, exposure phase, outcome, and fixture mutation check is classified
  deterministically. An illegal replacement marks the transfer conjunct
  protocol-failed and excludes it from attainment.
- Two independent blind contexts score every scored randomized normalized
  bundle, record a forced arm guess per bundle, and use the frozen blind resolver
  to record all disagreement citations.
- The report publishes every atomic and pair-aware count, absolute attainment,
  material delta, regression, arm-guess accuracy as a limitation, and distinct
  delivery/read/announcement/adherence observation.
- The result is labelled complete, protocol-failed, or unavailable exactly as
  frozen. Missing, unavailable, protocol-failed, and unblindable data never
  contribute to the transfer conjunct, which remains open; the result makes no
  same-case, population, reader-outcome, or wording-causation claim.

## Notes

The transfer result remains visible even when adverse. It is required for the
parent's cross-codebase and cross-language applicability clause but cannot be
pooled with the larger same-case row set or used to change treatment bytes.
