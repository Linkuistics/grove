# acceptance-replication-authority-k76

## Goal

Decide whether and under what evidential rule a post-failure replication of the
unchanged historical walkthrough-skill experiment may discharge the parent
behavioral acceptance claim.

## Context

- Clause-to-evidence trace and recommendation:
  `acceptance-contract-reconciliation-k75`.
- Parent contract: `walkthrough-skill-delivery-k19` and the root brief.
- Negative historical verdict:
  `docs/evaluations/writing-code-walkthroughs/README.md`.
- Frozen instrument:
  `docs/evaluations/writing-code-walkthroughs/baseline/rubric.md`.
- No evaluated treatment or control context may run before this decision and
  the scheduled review-planning chain are settled.

     to discharge the behavioral clause independently if it meets the unchanged

     non-removable clause of any discharge;
  2. retain the current strict contract, record behavioral acceptance as failed,
     and prohibit recovery execution until later skill-revision/evaluation work

  3. explicitly amend the Case A access rule and parent acceptance clause so a

## Notes

The previous recommendation to authorize an exact-instrument replication is not

## Decisions (running log)

The parent acceptance contract will use option 3: amend the Case A access rule
and the parent behavioral-acceptance clause so that a newly frozen,
requirement-derived paired instrument replaces the unchanged-rubric requirement.
The new instrument is not an exact replication. The original failed result and
its Case A selection limitation remain contrary historical evidence rather than
being reinterpreted, repaired, or superseded as records.

The replacement Case A instrument will assign treatment by guaranteed delivery,
not by post-hoc discovery selection. Its user prompt remains byte-identical
between arms; before the model answers, the execution procedure preloads the
verified final skill bytes into every enabled context, while the control
receives no skill. A failed or mismatched preload invalidates the apparatus, but
failure to follow the delivered instructions remains a valid treatment outcome.
Implicit skill discovery is outside this behavioral verdict and may be reported
separately as supplemental deployment evidence.

Cross-codebase and cross-language transfer will remain separately reported
generality evidence, not an added acceptance conjunct. The parent acceptance
contract therefore remains unchanged on transfer. The frozen `fzf` probe cannot
discharge the replacement behavioral verdict; recovery and reporting work must
label it as generality evidence and reassess whether ten repetitions remain
proportionate to its evidential value. Any newly selected transfer probe is
supplemental under the same boundary.

The replacement behavioral verdict will cover all three core surfaces as
acceptance-critical: intake (Case A), source inventory and fragment planning
(Case B), and exposition and assurance (Case C). A repaired Case A result alone
cannot discharge the parent claims that the skill elicits the contract and
embodies the complete generic walkthrough method.

The overall behavioral verdict is the conjunction of three independently
predeclared surface verdicts. Cases A, B, and C must each pass; successes on one
surface cannot compensate for failure on another. An aggregate summary may be
reported, but it has no acceptance authority.

Each surface verdict will require both material improvement over its own
contemporaneous no-skill comparator and a predeclared absolute floor for enabled
behavior. The replacement instrument must freeze, before any evaluated context
runs, each surface's primary rows, essential no-regression rows, sample size,
invalid-run treatment, scoring procedure, comparative threshold, absolute
floor, and report endpoint. Planning may justify the numeric thresholds, but
cannot select or revise them after observing outcomes.

The preserved historical records remain the sole pre-skill baseline and the
original failed enabled result remains contrary historical evidence. The new
paired no-skill arms are contemporaneous comparators only. The replacement
instrument independently discharges the amended behavioral clause if and only
if all three surface verdicts pass; it does not combine with, repair, rescore,
or erase the historical campaign.

Requirement-derived rows and protocol rules affect the amended acceptance
verdict only when the replacement instrument's pre-run acceptance manifest
explicitly includes them under the parent wording changed by this decision.
Every later addition, alternate endpoint, exploratory analysis, discovery
measure, or transfer criterion remains supplemental and cannot rescue or weaken
the frozen acceptance verdict.

The agreed test seams are: (1) a pre-run acceptance manifest freezing the
verified skill digest, prompts, fixtures, row sets, sample and replacement
rules, scoring, comparative thresholds, absolute floors, and three-surface AND
endpoint; (2) execution evidence that the verified skill was preloaded in every
enabled context, absent from controls, and paired with a byte-identical user
prompt; (3) fresh paired contexts preserving arm assignment, manifests, raw
events, invalid-attempt evidence, and access audits; (4) blind adjudication of
only frozen rows, with an independent rescore of every acceptance-critical
surface; and (5) separate Case A, B, and C verdicts feeding the
non-compensating final verdict while historical evidence remains immutable and
separately reported.

Evaluation apparatus must remain proportionate. When a bounded action is
simpler for the human to perform than a comprehensive or reusable test harness
would be to build, the plan should ask the human to perform that action instead
of automating it by default.

A human-performed execution step may produce acceptance evidence when its exact
inputs, procedure, outputs, and operator declaration are preserved in the
campaign record. This is an execution alternative, not a scoring shortcut:
predeclared rows, blind adjudication, independent rescoring, thresholds, and
verdict rules remain unchanged. The treatment-delivery seam therefore requires
auditable preload and arm-assignment evidence, whether captured by minimal
automation or by a documented human procedure; it does not require a reusable
harness when one would cost more than the bounded action it replaces.

The exact-instrument `recovery-campaign-k54` subtree is decided against and will
be pruned in place, preserving its files as abandoned history.
`supplemental-evaluation-k77` becomes the planning owner for the replacement
paired acceptance instrument; its unchanged stable handle does not limit the
explicit authority granted by the amended parent wording.
