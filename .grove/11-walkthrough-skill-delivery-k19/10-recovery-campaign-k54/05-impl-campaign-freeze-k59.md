# campaign-freeze-k59

## Goal

Turn the tested design, apparatus, and exact historical transfer artifacts into
one immutable pre-execution acceptance contract.

## Context

- Inputs: `measurement-design-k55`, `campaign-runner-k56`,
  `campaign-auditor-k57`, and `transfer-freeze-k58`.
- Requirements authority: `acceptance-replication-authority`.
- Deployed treatment:
  `plugins/linkuistics/skills/writing-code-walkthroughs/SKILL.md`.

## Done when

- One signed manifest proves exact identity with the historical rubric and
  prior transfer artifacts and pins the treatment, templates, apparatus,
  schedule, tests, and runtime identity.
- A machine-checkable exclusion list refuses every new row, gate, exposure
  classifier, resource-window rule, scorer rule, arm-guess probe, outcome rule,
  or target that belongs to `supplemental-evaluation`.
- The deployed skill is pinned by recursive manifest and aggregate digest; the
  historical instrument and records remain unchanged, and the original failed
  enabled campaign remains an explicit input to final synthesis.
- Stubbed end-to-end tests prove the historical no-tool/access rules,
  replacement ceiling, scoring contract, and incomplete-sample semantics.
  Altered-instrument and relabelled-control fixtures fail.
- One fresh adversarial reviewer receives only the complete freeze candidate
  and parent contract. Every finding is classified and settled before the
  manifest is signed; a second substantive review need is externalized through
  Grove rather than absorbed.
- No live evaluated treatment or control output exists before the freeze
  commit. Any later change to a frozen byte fails closed and requires a new
  predeclared cycle rather than an amendment in place.

## Notes

This is the temporal freeze boundary. Earlier leaves create drafts and tested
machinery; only this commit authorizes behavioral execution.
