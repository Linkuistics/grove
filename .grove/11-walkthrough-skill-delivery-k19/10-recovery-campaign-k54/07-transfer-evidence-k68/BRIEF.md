# transfer-evidence-k68 — brief

## Goal

Run and judge the exact previously frozen transfer probe as the historical
transfer claim.

## Done when

- Five enabled and five contemporaneous-comparator repetitions follow the exact
  historical transfer schedule, prompt, fzf fixture/revision, criteria,
  thresholds, regression, sampling, and scoring bytes without skill edits or
  post-outcome changes.
- Full access/outcome and replacement-history auditing plus the historical blind
  adjudication contract publish every frozen row and required campaign record.
- The historical transfer verdict retains its frozen semantics and remains
  outside the same-case primary endpoint. This node selects no new target and
  authors no new row.

## Decomposition

`transfer-runs-k69` owns execution and immutable raw records;
`transfer-adjudication-k70` owns exact historical scoring and verdict.

## Pointers

- Joint immutable contract: `campaign-freeze-k59`

## Notes

endpoint. A miss or unavailable sample is reported, not repaired with a
target-specific instruction.
