  old baseline, original failed enabled result, historical sample shortfalls,
  superseded-digest limitation, and transfer shortfall. It identifies the
  authorized replication by its manifest, treatment digest, runtime, prompts,

  New no-skill runs are always named contemporaneous comparators, including when

  endpoint, regression, and incomplete-sample semantics. New absolute/per-family
  gates, expanded regressions, pair-aware rules, arm guesses, and fail-closed

  verdict inputs.
- The exact historical transfer instrument receives its own verdict. This
  campaign reports no new transfer target.
- Skill bytes, discovery counts, deterministic guarantees, invalid-attempt
  records, and judged answer behavior remain distinct under the historical
  reporting contract.
- The report states whether the authorized replication met the unchanged rubric
  against both the historical baseline and contemporaneous comparator, whether
  that result has authority to discharge the parent clause, and whether current-
  digest deterministic checks passed.
- Claims are limited to the bounded executions; no visible read is called
  comprehension or use, and no sample threshold is generalized to readers,
  models, codebases, or future service versions.
- No skill refinement, old-output rescore, frozen-rubric rewrite, historical-
  result replacement, comparator relabelling, supplemental-row promotion, or
  target-shaped rule is hidden in synthesis.
