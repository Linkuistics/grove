# recovery-campaign-k54 — brief

## Goal

Run only the exact-historical-instrument recovery evaluation authorized by
`acceptance-replication-authority`, while preserving the accepted historical
experiment as the authority for its pre-skill baseline and unchanged rubric.

## Context

The clause-to-evidence trace in `acceptance-contract-reconciliation-k75` is the
campaign's claim boundary. The historical report and rubric remain evidence
about their frozen experiment. This subtree may replicate that exact instrument
only with explicit requirements authority. `supplemental-evaluation` owns every
new instrument and runs only after this subtree.

## Done when

- The requirements decision explicitly authorizes the evidential role of any
  new replication. Without that authorization the subtree launches no evaluated
  context and cannot retire through a behavioral acceptance claim.
- One joint freeze made before treatment execution references or byte-verifies
  every historical instrument artifact and contains no new behavioral rule.
- The acceptance manifest pins the frozen rubric digest and the exact historical
  Case A/B/C prompts, fixtures, atomic rows, sample and invalid-run rules,
  scoring, `R`, `G`, materiality threshold, endpoint, and regression rule. The
  prior transfer prompt, fixture, criteria, thresholds, and regression rule are
  equally exact if the historical transfer shortfall is rerun.
- Every row or rule absent from the historical instrument is excluded and
  routed to `supplemental-evaluation`; it cannot enter this subtree's files,
  schedules, scores, or verdict.
- Each same-case surface has five enabled repetitions and five contemporaneous
  no-skill repetitions in the historical ABBA/BAAB order. Invalidity,
  replacement, the two-replacement ceiling, and sample shortfalls follow the
  frozen rubric exactly.
- Case A and C tool calls remain invalid; Case B retains its declared-fixture
  read-only boundary. Skill discovery and use therefore remain observations or
  limitations under the historical instrument, never reasons to change access
  or sample membership.
- Scope elicitation, source/fragments, and exposition/assurance each publish the
  unchanged-rubric result. Historical missing data retains the historical
  rubric's meaning.
- The same-case source/fragment surface uses the historical external OCaml
  fixture at its frozen digest, so the primary evidence includes a non-Rust,
  cross-codebase case.
- The exact historical transfer probe, if authorized for replication, publishes
  its unchanged verdict. This subtree never selects a new target.
- Current-digest campaign checks, plugin structure/installation checks, and
  applicable repository checks publish a terminal pass, failed, or unavailable
  record independently of judged model behavior; anything but pass leaves the
  deterministic parent conjunct open.

## Decomposition

- `acceptance-replication-authority` first decides whether a post-failure
  replication may discharge the parent claim and which contract governs it.
- `measurement-design-k55` writes the exact-instrument conformance contract.
- `campaign-runner-k56` builds the sealed historical-schedule runner and complete record
  machinery without treatment calls.
- `campaign-auditor-k57` builds the access/outcome classifier and blinded
  scoring-bundle seam.
- `transfer-freeze-k58` proves exact identity of the historical transfer probe.
- `campaign-freeze-k59` reconciles and jointly freezes all tested artifacts.
- `same-case-evidence-k60` produces the three same-case records and primary
  verdict after the freeze.
- `transfer-evidence-k68` produces the separately labelled transfer record.
- `deployment-verification-k71` proves deterministic deployment properties at
  the frozen digest; `campaign-report-k72` publishes the bounded synthesis.

## Notes

The campaign tests the deployed bytes and performs no skill refinement. A byte
change before or during execution invalidates this freeze and becomes new tree
work; it is never absorbed as an in-campaign treatment update. The original
failed enabled result is always reported and is never overwritten, pooled, or
selected away. Recovery no-skill runs are called contemporaneous comparators,
never the pre-skill baseline. All three same-case generation slices complete
before any same-case adjudication result is published.
