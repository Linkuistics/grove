# supplemental-evaluation-k77

## Goal

Plan a separately frozen contemporaneous evaluation that applies the valid
F1–F14 recovery protections and produces bounded supplemental evidence without
claiming to be the historical pre-skill experiment or unchanged-rubric verdict.

## Context

- Run only after `recovery-campaign-k54` has published or dispositioned the
  exact-instrument recovery, so the acceptance result cannot be influenced by
  supplemental outcomes.
- Claim boundary and clause trace:
  `acceptance-contract-reconciliation-k75`.
- Protocol findings and integrated dispositions:
  `evaluation-recovery-k73` and `evaluation-recovery-k74`.
- Historical evidence remains immutable:
  `docs/evaluations/writing-code-walkthroughs/README.md` and
  `docs/evaluations/writing-code-walkthroughs/baseline/rubric.md`.

## Done when

- Find the smallest independently useful supplemental increments before cutting
  execution leaves; do not recreate the acceptance campaign inside this tree.
- Predeclare every new prompt, fixture, atomic row, target/regression set,
  threshold, endpoint, mixed-row rule, exposure classifier, replacement resource
  window, scorer rule, arm-guess probe, and fail-closed outcome under a
  `supplemental` namespace before any evaluated context runs.
- Apply F1–F14 or record a requirements-grounded reason a finding no longer
  applies. In particular, preserve generation-before-adjudication, deterministic
  replacement legality, criterion-author role separation, exhaustive regression
  coverage, scorer/resolver ownership, dual scoring, arm guesses, adjacent pair
  execution, and bounded non-selective resumption.
- State that the historical records remain the only pre-skill baseline. New
  no-skill runs are contemporaneous controls for this supplemental instrument
  and may not be relabelled or pooled with the historical counts.
- Keep every supplemental score, denominator, verdict, transfer target, and
  report section separate from the unchanged-rubric result. A supplemental pass
  cannot rescue an acceptance failure, and a supplemental failure cannot rewrite
  a historical score.
- Cut a new transfer probe only when its separately stated generality question
  earns the cost; never present it as closure of the frozen fzf shortfall.

## Notes

This workstream exists because the stronger recovery controls are independently
useful, but they alter the accepted instrument. Its product is diagnostic and
generality evidence for future requirements or skill revisions, not evidence
that the parent brief's unchanged-rubric clause passed.
