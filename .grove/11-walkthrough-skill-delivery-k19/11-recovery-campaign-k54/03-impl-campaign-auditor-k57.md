# campaign-auditor-k57

## Goal

Make historical access validity, assignment outcome, and scorer blinding
reproducible over the runner's preserved records.

## Context

- Measurement draft: `measurement-design-k55`.
- Runner record contract: `campaign-runner-k56`.

## Done when

- The auditor applies the historical Case A/C no-tool rule and Case B declared-
  fixture rule exactly. It does not add a legal skill-directory read or a new
  command grammar.
- The auditor preserves the historical startup manifests, enabled-skill digest,
  raw events, and per-case discovery count. It adds no new delivery/read/
  announcement/adherence taxonomy to the acceptance verdict.
- Tests replay complete attempt histories and prove replacement legality follows
  only the historical rubric. An exposure-derived or supplemental classifier is
  refused before scoring.
- Full events feed the historical access audit and the historical randomized
  scoring bundle exactly as frozen.
- Fixture tests prove every Case A/C tool call is invalid and Case B permits only
  the historical declared-fixture read boundary; a `SKILL.md` read retains the
  historical case-specific outcome.
- Scoring matches the historical rubric's adjudication contract exactly. Extra
  scorers, forced arm guesses, and new disagreement rules are out of scope.
- Tests cover every historical invalid-run predicate, valid refusals and poor
  answers, timeouts, missing finals, truncation, the two-replacement ceiling,
  sample shortfall, randomized arm labels, and the frozen adjudication rule.
- Documentation states that the audit covers observable model-requested access,
  not operating-system filesystem unreadability or every library file an
  allowed executable touches.

## Notes

The auditor classifies evidence already produced by the runner. It never edits
raw streams or supplies treatment bytes to a behavioral scorer. All prohibited-
access and scorer-invocation fixtures are synthesized event records; this leaf
launches no live evaluated treatment or control model before the joint freeze.
