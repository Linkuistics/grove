# campaign-report-k72

## Goal

Publish the bounded recovery-cycle synthesis and state each parent clause's
status without substituting a new comparator or supplemental instrument for the
accepted historical experiment.

This exact-replication reporting path is abandoned under requirements option 3.
The replacement campaign report must preserve the same historical limitations,
label transfer only as generality evidence, and apply the newly frozen paired
instrument without presenting it as an exact replication.

## Done when

- The durable report reproduces the clause-to-evidence trace and preserves the
  old baseline, original failed enabled result, historical sample shortfalls,
  superseded-digest limitation, and transfer shortfall. It identifies the
  authorized replication by its manifest, treatment digest, runtime, prompts,
  criteria, and chronology.
- If requirements option 1 authorized an exact replication, the report carries
  the non-removable Case A limitation beside those limitations: the enabled arm
  is selected against observable treatment use, so a pass may discharge the
  rubric comparison but cannot establish causal skill use or effect.
- The historical pre-skill records are named as the sole pre-skill baseline.
  New no-skill runs are always named contemporaneous comparators, including when
  they supply the second operand required by the frozen endpoint.
- The report includes only exact historical-rubric rows, `R`, `G`, materiality,
  endpoint, regression, and incomplete-sample semantics. New absolute/per-family
  gates, expanded regressions, pair-aware rules, arm guesses, and fail-closed
  outcomes belong to `supplemental-evaluation` and do not appear as campaign
  verdict inputs.
- Cases B and C publish descriptive per-row counts and terminal shortfall
  records only. Their materiality and regression classifications are printed as
  `undefined`, never zero, miss, or omission.
- The exact historical transfer instrument receives its own verdict or
  separately reported generality result according to the transfer contract
  settled by `acceptance-replication-authority`. The report does not promote a
  parent `Context` sentence into an acceptance conjunct and reports no new
  transfer target.
- Skill bytes, discovery counts, deterministic guarantees, invalid-attempt
  records, and judged answer behavior remain distinct under the historical
  reporting contract.
- The report states whether the authorized replication met the unchanged rubric
  against both the historical baseline and contemporaneous comparator, whether
  that result has authority to discharge the parent clause, and whether current-
  digest deterministic checks passed.
- Claims are limited to the bounded executions; no visible read is called
  comprehension or use, and no sample threshold is generalized to readers,
  models, codebases, or future service versions.
- No skill refinement, old-output rescore, frozen-rubric rewrite, historical-
  result replacement, comparator relabelling, supplemental-row promotion, or
  target-shaped rule is hidden in synthesis.
