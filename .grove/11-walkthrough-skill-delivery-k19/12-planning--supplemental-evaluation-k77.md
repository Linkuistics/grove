Plan the separately frozen paired instrument authorized to discharge the
amended parent behavioral-acceptance clause, while preserving the historical
experiment as immutable contrary evidence.

  no evaluated context may run from that subtree.

  `acceptance-replication-authority-k76`.

## Done when

- Cut the smallest independently useful increments for one accepted paired
  campaign covering intake, source/fragment planning, and exposition/assurance;
  all three generation increments precede every adjudication increment.
- Before any evaluated context runs, freeze one acceptance manifest containing
  the verified treatment digest, prompts, fixtures, per-surface primary and
  no-regression rows, sample and replacement rules, scoring, comparative
  thresholds, absolute floors, and the three-surface AND endpoint.
- Guarantee treatment delivery by preloading the verified final skill bytes in
  every enabled context while controls receive no skill and user prompts remain
  byte-identical. A preload failure invalidates the apparatus; failure to follow
  delivered instructions remains a valid outcome.
- Apply F1–F14 or record a requirements-grounded reason a finding no longer
  applies. In particular, preserve generation-before-adjudication, deterministic
  replacement legality, criterion-author role separation, exhaustive regression
  coverage, scorer/resolver ownership, blind independent rescoring of every
  acceptance-critical surface, arm guesses where they do not disclose an arm
  before judgment, adjacent pair execution, and bounded non-selective
  resumption.
- State that the historical records remain the only pre-skill baseline. New
  no-skill runs are contemporaneous comparators for the replacement instrument
  and may not be relabelled or pooled with historical counts. The new instrument
  independently discharges the amended clause only when all three surface
  verdicts pass; it never combines with, repairs, or erases the failed campaign.
- Keep every row or protocol rule outside the frozen acceptance manifest under
  a supplemental namespace. Supplemental outcomes, implicit-discovery measures,
  alternate endpoints, and transfer evidence cannot rescue or weaken the
  accepted verdict.
- Prefer a documented bounded human action over a comprehensive reusable
  harness when it is simpler. Human execution can produce evidence only when
  its exact inputs, procedure, outputs, and operator declaration are preserved;
  blind scoring and frozen verdict authority remain independent.
- Cut a new transfer probe only when its separately stated generality question
  earns the cost. Treat the frozen `fzf` probe only as generality evidence and
  reassess whether ten repetitions are proportionate; a new target is
  supplemental and never acceptance-critical.
- As the final planning action, commission a lazy `review-planning` sibling with
  this leaf's bare stem and a charter to try to disprove the manifest boundary,
  treatment-delivery rule, three surface verdicts, and proportional apparatus
  before any live-model execution.

## Notes

This handle retains the word `supplemental` because Grove has no retitle verb in
the installed build. The amended parent wording explicitly promotes only the
pre-run manifest's three-surface paired verdict to acceptance authority;
everything else produced here remains supplemental.
