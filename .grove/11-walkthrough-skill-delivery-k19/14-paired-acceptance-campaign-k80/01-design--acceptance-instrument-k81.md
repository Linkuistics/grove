# acceptance-instrument-k81

## Goal

Author the complete requirement-derived acceptance instrument without access
to treatment wording, historical row definitions, or campaign outcomes.

## Context

- Authority: `acceptance-replication-authority-k76` and the parent brief chain.
- Generic method contract: the accepted walkthrough-method requirements, not
  the deployed skill bytes.

## Done when

- A criterion-author role receives only the parent behavioral contract and the
  generic walkthrough method. Its exact inputs, raw output, runtime identity,
  chronology, and digests are preserved; it receives no skill bytes,
  historical rubric rows, historical outputs, or new campaign outcomes.
- One draft manifest freezes byte-exact control/treatment user prompts and
  fixtures for intake, external source/fragment planning, and
  exposition/assurance. The source/fragment fixture is external and non-Rust.
- Every requirement-derived behavior is assigned exactly once to a surface and
  to either that surface's primary set or exhaustive no-regression set. The
  mixed-control judging rule is predeclared and cannot drop a row after counts
  are known.
- Each surface fixes its sample size, paired counterbalanced order, valid and
  invalid outcome rules, deterministic replacement rule, bounded resource
  budget, pair-atomic non-selective resumption, binary scoring, material
  comparative threshold, and absolute enabled-performance floor.
- Missing, unavailable, protocol-failed, and unblindable evidence is explicitly
  fail-closed. It never contributes to attainment and leaves the relevant
  surface and parent conjunct open.
- The overall endpoint is the conjunction of three independent surface passes;
  aggregation is descriptive only and no surface can compensate for another.
- The delivery contract says enabled contexts are preloaded with the later
  verified skill bytes before the user prompt and controls receive no skill.
  Prompt bytes are identical between arms. Preload failure invalidates the
  apparatus; non-adherence after valid delivery is a retained outcome.
- Acceptance-critical rows and rules occupy the manifest's acceptance
  namespace. Arm guesses, implicit-discovery observations, alternate endpoints,
  transfer evidence, and exploratory measures occupy a frozen `supplemental`
  namespace and have no verdict authority.
- The treatment-to-row coverage map and historical retained/reworded/dropped
  comparison are expressly deferred to `campaign-freeze-k84`; neither later
  map may add, remove, or weaken a row.
- No evaluated treatment or control context runs in this leaf.

## Notes

Prefer the smallest criterion set that exhaustively covers the amended parent
behavioral contract. Difficulty scoring a row is a design problem, not grounds
to omit an accepted requirement.
