# acceptance-instrument-k81

## Goal

Author the complete requirement-derived acceptance instrument without access
to treatment wording, historical row definitions, or campaign outcomes.

## Context

- Authority: `acceptance-replication-authority-k76` and the parent brief chain.
- Delivery authority: the settled and reviewed `delivery-channel-authority-k94`
  decision. This leaf cannot proceed if that decision leaves the behavioral
  conjunct open for want of a checkable channel.
- Generic method contract: the accepted walkthrough-method requirements, not
  the deployed skill bytes.

## Done when

- A criterion-author role runs through the delivery authority's sealed,
  tool-free fresh-request seam. It receives only the parent behavioral contract
  and the generic walkthrough method. Its exact effective request, declared
  empty tool set, provider acknowledgement, raw output, runtime identity,
  chronology, and digests are preserved as machine-checkable isolation
  evidence. It receives no skill bytes, historical rubric rows, historical
  outputs, repository access, or new campaign outcomes.
- One draft manifest freezes byte-exact control/treatment user prompts and
  fixtures for intake, external source/fragment planning, and
  exposition/assurance. The source/fragment fixture is external and non-Rust.
- Every requirement-derived behavior is assigned exactly once to a surface and
  to either that surface's primary set or exhaustive no-regression set. The
  mixed-control judging rule is predeclared and cannot drop a row after counts
  are known.
- Each surface fixes its sample size, paired counterbalanced order, valid and
  invalid outcome taxonomy, deterministic replacement rule, bounded resource
  windows, pair-atomic non-selective resumption, binary scoring, material
  comparative threshold, and absolute enabled-performance floor. The draft
  publishes each surface's `n`, a best-case attainability calculation proving
  both gates can be met, and the campaign's total evaluated-context count.
- Missing, unavailable, protocol-failed, and unblindable evidence is explicitly
  fail-closed. It never contributes to attainment and leaves the relevant
  surface and parent conjunct open.
- The overall endpoint is the conjunction of three independent surface passes;
  aggregation is descriptive only and no surface can compensate for another.
- The delivery contract imports the reviewed `delivery-channel-authority-k94`
  mechanism without reinterpretation: it names the exact effective-request
  treatment field, authoritative provider acknowledgement, receipt artifacts,
  exhaustive treatment transport, freshness proof, and failure modes. Enabled
  requests carry the verified treatment before the byte-identical user message;
  controls omit it. A missing or mismatched authoritative receipt is an
  apparatus failure; non-adherence after valid delivery is retained behavior.
- Tool access is independent of delivery and fixed by the reviewed authority:
  intake and exposition/assurance expose no tools; source/fragment planning
  exposes only one manifest-bound fixture-read operation with no caller-supplied
  path. No skill read or discovery call is delivery evidence.
- The outcome taxonomy is keyed only by preserved machine signals: a carrier
  failure emits no assistant token (or is a harness-level nonzero/transport
  failure with no model output), a behavioral outcome contains any model output
  including refusal, omission, non-adherence, or a model-initiated boundary
  attempt, and an apparatus failure is a pre-submission delivery or manifest
  mismatch. Only carrier failures are replaceable. Behavioral outcomes are
  retained unconditionally; apparatus failure invalidates the campaign.
- The resource rule freezes an initial global allocation plus a fixed number
  and size of automatic replenishment windows, reserved by surface. A window is
  opened automatically only between whole pairs. If the final reserved window
  cannot fund both arms, the pair and surface become unavailable without
  soliciting another budget. Mid-pair exhaustion voids the whole pair as
  unavailable and neither arm enters the sample or resumes in isolation; later
  surfaces continue from their own frozen reservations unless the whole
  apparatus is invalid.
- Generation continues through every scheduled surface after a prior surface
  becomes protocol-failed or unavailable. Only a campaign-wide apparatus
  failure stops later submission, so no operator chooses whether to continue
  after seeing an adverse behavioral record.
- This is the one acceptance-authorized campaign for its treatment digest. A
  later campaign at the same digest is supplemental unless a new requirements
  decision grants acceptance authority; a revised treatment digest is a new
  requirements question, not an automatic retry.
- The scoring prompt, scorer runtime declaration, and blind disagreement rule
  are authored here under the criterion author's restricted-input and
  preserved-chronology regime. `campaign-apparatus-k82` may invoke but not
  rewrite them, and `campaign-freeze-k84` pins them.
- The external non-Rust fixture is selected, obtained, and vendored by this
  leaf with origin, revision, path, and digest recorded. Evaluated contexts use
  only the vendored bytes; no evaluated run retrieves a fixture over a network.
- Acceptance-critical rows and rules occupy the manifest's `acceptance`
  namespace. Arm guesses, implicit-discovery observations, predeclared alternate
  endpoints, and transfer observations occupy a frozen `supplemental` namespace
  and have no verdict authority. A third `exploratory` namespace opens only
  after the accepted verdict for explicitly post-hoc analyses; it carries no
  pre-registration or verdict authority.
- The treatment-to-row coverage map and historical retained/reworded/dropped
  comparison are expressly deferred to `campaign-freeze-k84`; neither later
  map may add, remove, or weaken a row.
- No evaluated treatment or control context runs in this leaf.

## Notes

Prefer the smallest criterion set that exhaustively covers the amended parent
behavioral contract. Difficulty scoring a row is a design problem, not grounds
to omit an accepted requirement.
