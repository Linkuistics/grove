# paired-acceptance-campaign-k80 — brief

## Goal

Freeze and run one requirement-derived paired campaign that can independently
discharge the amended behavioral-acceptance clause for intake,
source/fragment planning, and exposition/assurance without changing or pooling
the historical experiment.

## Done when

- One manifest committed before any evaluated context pins the verified
  treatment digest, prompts, fixtures, acceptance rows, sample and replacement
  rules, scoring, comparative thresholds, absolute floors, runtime controls,
  and the non-compensating three-surface endpoint.
- The manifest separates frozen `acceptance` authority from a frozen
  `supplemental` namespace and reserves a visibly post-verdict `exploratory`
  namespace. Predeclared discovery observations, scorer arm guesses, alternate
  analyses, and transfer observations are supplemental; post-hoc analyses are
  exploratory. Neither can rescue or weaken the accepted verdict.
- This campaign consumes the settled and reviewed
  `delivery-channel-authority-k94` decision without reinterpretation. Every
  enabled effective request carries the verified final treatment before its
  byte-identical user message; every control omits it. The authority defines the
  provider acknowledgement and preserved request bytes that constitute a
  machine-checkable delivery receipt. A missing or mismatched receipt invalidates
  the apparatus, while any model output after valid delivery remains behavior.
  Intake and exposition expose no tools; source/fragment exposes only the
  authority's one manifest-bound fixture-read operation, so delivery never
  depends on skill discovery. If the authority leaves the channel unresolved,
  this node does not run.
- Pair arms run back-to-back in frozen counterbalanced order. A deterministic
  carrier / behavioral / apparatus taxonomy governs replacement legality:
  carrier failures with no model output alone are replaceable, every model
  output is retained, and pre-submission delivery mismatch invalidates the
  apparatus. Globally bounded resource windows replenish automatically only
  between whole pairs under non-selective frozen order; terminal exhaustion
  voids the whole pair and makes its surface unavailable without admitting or
  resuming one arm alone or opening discretion.
- Every generation surface runs despite an earlier surface's protocol failure
  or unavailability; only shared apparatus invalidity stops later submission.
- Intake, source/fragment, and exposition/assurance generation all complete
  before any adjudication. Each surface then receives two independent blind
  scores for every bundle and a frozen blind-resolution procedure.
- Each surface passes only by both materially improving over its own
  contemporaneous no-skill comparator and clearing its absolute enabled floor.
  Missing, unavailable, protocol-failed, or unblindable evidence cannot attain
  a conjunct; the campaign passes only when all three surfaces pass.
- Historical records remain the sole pre-skill baseline and the failed
  historical campaign remains immutable contrary evidence. New control arms
  are contemporaneous comparators only and are never pooled with historical
  counts.
- New campaign artifacts live only under
  `docs/evaluations/writing-code-walkthroughs-paired-acceptance/`. A recursive
  digest manifest of `docs/evaluations/writing-code-walkthroughs/` is captured
  at freeze and reverified at final verdict so historical immutability is an
  executed check.
- Deterministic skill/plugin checks pass at the exact treatment digest used by
  the campaign. Before execution, the scheduled review chain may repair the
  candidate only by committing a complete replacement manifest with a new
  digest; execution binds to the final reviewed manifest. No frozen-byte
  amendment occurs after that boundary. This is the one acceptance-authorized
  campaign per treatment digest; another same-digest campaign is supplemental
  absent a new requirements decision.

## Decomposition

- `acceptance-instrument-k81` consumes `delivery-channel-authority-k94` and
  derives the three surfaces, outcome/resource
  rules, scorer instrument, attainability proof, and verdict rules in a sealed
  no-tool criterion-author context independently of treatment bytes and new
  outcomes; it also vendors the external fixture.
- `campaign-apparatus-k82` implements only the deterministic seams and bounded
  operator procedure needed to preserve paired execution and blind scoring. It
  is an expected `leaf-decompose` candidate at the execution-core versus
  adjudication-support seam and does not automate verdict arithmetic.
- `treatment-verification-k83` proves the final deployable skill bytes,
  authority-defined transport, and control/treatment effective-request delta.
- `campaign-freeze-k84` reconciles those artifacts into the sole immutable
  manifest and commissions its lazy adversarial review before execution.
- `intake-generation-k85`, `source-fragment-generation-k86`, and
  `exposition-assurance-generation-k87` produce complete raw paired records in
  that order without publishing scores.
- `intake-adjudication-k88`, `source-fragment-adjudication-k89`, and
  `exposition-assurance-adjudication-k90` independently score the frozen
  surfaces only after all generation is complete.
- `three-surface-verdict-k91` applies the AND endpoint, reports historical and
  supplemental evidence separately, and states the bounded parent result.
