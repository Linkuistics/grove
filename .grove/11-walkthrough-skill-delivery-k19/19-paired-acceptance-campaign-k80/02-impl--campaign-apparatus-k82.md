# campaign-apparatus-k82

## Goal

Build the minimum auditable execution, access-audit, and blind-scoring seams
needed to run the frozen paired instrument without a comprehensive reusable
harness.

## Context

- Instrument draft: `acceptance-instrument-k81`.
- Historical failure modes: F1-F3 and F9-F13 in
  `evaluation-recovery-k73`.

## Done when

- A bounded operator procedure plus only the small deterministic helpers it
  earns accepts manifest data rather than embedding a surface. If a documented
  human action is simpler, it records exact inputs, procedure, outputs, and an
  operator declaration instead of adding reusable automation.
- Each attempt uses the reviewed `delivery-channel-authority-k94` fresh-request
  mechanism and a preserved run directory. The launcher builds the exact
  provider request, enabled treatment field or control omission, byte-identical
  user message, and per-surface tool declaration that authority defines.
  Preserved effective-request bytes, provider acknowledgement, treatment and
  prompt digests, and raw event stream are the delivery receipt; installed
  homes, command arguments, and later skill discovery are not receipts.
- A missing or mismatched authoritative receipt marks the apparatus invalid and
  launches no acceptance-bearing continuation. Intake and exposition/assurance
  declare no tools. Source/fragment exposes only the authority's one
  manifest-bound fixture-read operation; its handler accepts no path, verifies
  the vendored fixture digest, and cannot traverse, mutate, or access a network.
- Pair assignment is precommitted and counterbalanced. The two arms of each
  pair execute back-to-back with start/end timestamps under one pair id.
- Replacement eligibility is a deterministic function of the frozen outcome
  taxonomy and preserved signals, restoring `evaluation-recovery-k74` Doubt D3
  at the stronger carrier/behavior/apparatus seam. A carrier failure has no
  assistant token or model output and is replaceable; any model output is a
  retained behavioral outcome; a delivery-authority manifest or receipt mismatch
  is a campaign-wide apparatus failure. Automatic globally bounded resource
  windows open only between whole pairs in earliest-incomplete order, cannot
  depend on arm or outcome, and never separate a pair. Final-window exhaustion
  marks the pair and surface unavailable with no discretionary refill. If it
  occurs mid-pair, neither arm enters the sample and the other arm never resumes
  alone; later surfaces use only their frozen reservations.
- The auditor replays the complete attempt history before scoring. An illegal
  replacement marks the affected surface protocol-failed under the frozen
  consequence and cannot be repaired by selecting another attempt.
- Full raw events feed access and validity auditing. Treatment-neutral bundles
  redact explicit arm metadata without discarding behavioral evidence, and
  every scored bundle is sent to two independent blind scorers. A separately
  owned blind resolver applies only the frozen disagreement rule.
- Each scorer records a forced arm guess after scoring and before labels are
  revealed. Scorer and resolver contexts are fresh per surface, and no arm label
  or guess-accuracy result is revealed until all three surface score records are
  sealed. Guess accuracy is then emitted under the supplemental namespace and
  is never a filter or verdict operand.
- Synthesized fixtures and stub executables cover delivery-receipt mismatch, every
  outcome and replacement branch, resource exhaustion and resumption,
  pair-atomic scheduling, prohibited access, missing/truncated finals, bundle
  redaction, two-scorer invocation, and blind resolution without launching a
  live evaluated model.
- Any scripts follow the repository's applicable language conventions and pass
  their focused checks.

## Notes

The apparatus proves observable request and record boundaries, not operating-
system unreadability. Scoring helpers preserve evidence; they never rewrite raw
events or infer treatment adherence from delivery alone.

This leaf is an expected `leaf-decompose` candidate. If implementation exceeds
one focused session, split first at the independently useful seam between (1)
the deterministic execution core — delivery receipt, assignment, outcome
classification, replacement replay, resource resumption, and record validation
— and (2) adjudication support — bundle formation/redaction, scorer and resolver
invocation, and access auditing. Do not build a verdict-calculation helper:
surface leaves publish per-row counts, and readers apply the frozen formula.
