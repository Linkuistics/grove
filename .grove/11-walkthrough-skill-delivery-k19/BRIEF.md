# walkthrough-skill-delivery-k19 — brief

## Goal

Turn the validated walkthrough method into a generic deployable Linkuistics
skill whose behavior is demonstrated against a no-skill baseline.

## Context

The skill belongs at
`plugins/linkuistics/skills/writing-code-walkthroughs`. It must apply across
codebases and languages; `ordinal-fs-tree` supplies evidence and a worked
development history, not skill-specific vocabulary.

## Done when

- The committed historical no-skill scenarios remain the sole pre-skill
  baseline and expose the relevant Case A failures; the Case B/C shortfalls and
  the original failed enabled result remain explicit contrary evidence.
- The skill elicits target, included source, audience, depth, output form, style,
  and verification requirements one question at a time before authoring.
- The body captures the proven method for concept ordering, source inventory,
  fragment ownership/tangling, self-contained prose, scoped and final checks,
  and independent technical/editorial review without copying this book's domain.
- Skill frontmatter, progressive disclosure, citations, harness declaration,
  plugin layout, and description follow the Linkuistics authoring conventions.
- A new requirement-derived paired instrument freezes its prompts, fixtures,
  rows, sample and replacement rules, scoring, comparative thresholds,
  absolute floors, and endpoint before any evaluated context runs.
- Fresh paired contexts cover intake, source/fragment planning, and
  exposition/assurance. The verified final skill is preloaded in enabled
  contexts, absent from controls, and paired with byte-identical user prompts.
- Each surface independently shows material improvement over its
  contemporaneous no-skill comparator and clears its absolute enabled-
  performance floor; acceptance is the conjunction of all three verdicts.
- Plugin installation/structure checks and any skill-specific deterministic
  tests pass.

## Decomposition

- `skill-baseline-k20` defines and runs the no-skill control before skill bytes
  can influence the result.
- `writing-code-walkthroughs-k21` authors the smallest generic skill that
  addresses observed failures and preserves successful default behavior.
- `skill-evaluation-k22` reruns the same rubric with the skill enabled, closes
  demonstrated wording gaps, and proves deployment.
- The completed evaluation report remains a negative historical result.
  `acceptance-contract-reconciliation-k75`, its review chain, and
  `acceptance-replication-authority-k76` establish why an exact-instrument retry
  has no acceptance authority and preserve that result unchanged.
- `recovery-campaign-k54` is abandoned as the exact-replication path.
  `supplemental-evaluation-k77` now plans the replacement paired acceptance
  instrument, applies the relevant F1–F14 protections, and keeps discovery and
  transfer work outside the primary verdict.

## Notes

Predeclare each replacement scenario's prompt, observable behaviors, scoring
rule, sample size, contamination controls, comparative threshold, and absolute
floor. Preserve generation-before-adjudication and distinguish deterministic
checks from judgment scored over agent outputs. Use the least apparatus that
leaves the evidence auditable: a documented human execution may replace a
comprehensive one-use harness when its inputs, procedure, outputs, and operator
declaration are preserved, but scoring and verdict rules remain independent.

`skill-baseline-k20` and `skill-evaluation-k22` are expected
`leaf-decompose` candidates split by scenario group when the campaign exceeds a
focused session. The first baseline child commits the rubric once; all remaining
baseline and enabled-evaluation children share it unchanged.

The baseline completed under the frozen invalid-run rule with behavioral
evidence only for scope elicitation. The source/fragment and
exposition/assurance cases each exhausted their replacement allowance without a
final answer, so downstream authoring must not infer failures from those cases;
the replacement evaluation preserves those historical shortfalls while using
its separately frozen instrument.
