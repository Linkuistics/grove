# grove.avoid-endless-decomposition-loops — brief

## Goal

Update Grove's planning and decomposition guidance so that sessions make
verifiable progress toward the smallest useful working outcome. Deliver that
increment, then improve it in subsequent groves, rather than seeking perfection
or endlessly deferring the same work through decomposition and review cycles.

## Done when

The updated rules address the failure patterns observed in live groves,
preserve useful decomposition and review, and have been checked against
the four recorded failure patterns and positive examples of bounded delivery,
necessary preparation, defect repair and review.

## Agreed behavior

Make something useful work first. Each grove delivers a bounded increment with
proportionate checks; further improvements belong in subsequent groves rather
than indefinitely extending the current one.

When an agent repeatedly hands original acceptance work to a successor, keep
the current task open and attempt that acceptance. Keep necessary repairs in
that task. A further split needs a concrete explanation of how it advances
the goal. Report a real blocker explicitly rather than retiring the task as
success or minting another unchanged remainder. The human agreed this response
during plan-k1 on 2026-11-16.

Review creates more work only for defects blocking agreed acceptance or presenting
material risk. Record optional polish separately. Reopening reviewed work needs
a concrete change or new evidence. The human also agreed this boundary and the
incremental-delivery direction during plan-k1.

Preserve required safety and quality constraints. Where existing agreed acceptance
is too broad for a useful increment, report the scope decision explicitly rather
than quietly weakening acceptance. The four evidence groves remain read-only.

## Decomposition

The initial requirements and evidence are settled. One implementation leaf can
update the canonical methodology and planning guidance and verify the change.
No additional research, design or planning session is justified. Create review
only when the resulting artifact warrants independent challenge; do not queue
speculative follow-up hardening.

## Pointers

The two other live Grove project groves, APIAnyware, and Koine are the requested
sources of evidence.

plan-k1 records the four read-only examples and the rule interaction they expose.
Do not repeat that investigation unless a concrete unresolved question needs it.
It also records the bounded verification plan: the four observed patterns and
positive controls for useful delivery, necessary preparation and material repair.

## Notes

The human reports that multiple open groves repeatedly create or repeat tasks,
usually implementation and review, without advancing toward their goals.
