# SQLite

harness-dispatch compiles in SQLite, from the amalgamation that the
`libsqlite3-sys` crate bundles. SQLite's authors dedicate it to the public
domain (<https://sqlite.org/copyright.html>), and its source files carry this
notice in place of a licence:

> The author disclaims copyright to this source code.  In place of
> a legal notice, here is a blessing:
>
>    May you do good and not evil.
>    May you find forgiveness for yourself and forgive others.
