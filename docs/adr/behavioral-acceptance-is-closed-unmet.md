# Behavioral acceptance is closed as unmet

The `writing-code-walkthroughs` behavioral-acceptance conjunct is closed as
unmet. It required intake, source/fragment planning, and exposition/assurance to
each show predeclared material improvement over a contemporaneous no-skill
comparator and clear an absolute enabled-performance floor. The preserved
[historical evaluation](../evaluations/writing-code-walkthroughs/README.md)
remains contrary evidence; it is not repaired, rescored, or combined with a
replacement campaign.

The replacement campaign does not run. No inspected hosted provider documented
an attestation of the effective request at the final boundary before inference,
and controlled self-hosting was declined after its engineering and compute cost
was weighed against closing the conjunct. Installed skill bytes, command
construction, request identifiers, usage counts, client-side prompt captures,
and local file-read events are not substitutes for that final-boundary evidence.
The plugin's durable provenance record carries the same closure in
[`plugins/linkuistics/PROVENANCE.md`](../../plugins/linkuistics/PROVENANCE.md).

## Considered options

A hosted channel would become eligible if its provider documented and supplied
a content-bound final-boundary attestation. A controlled self-hosted runtime
would become eligible if a later requirements decision authorized provisioning
and independently observable capture of the complete bytes-to-tokens-to-inference
boundary. Neither condition held, so closure was chosen instead of leaving the
conjunct indefinitely pending.

## Consequences

The abandoned paired-campaign plan grants no authority to future work. A future
campaign requires a new requirements decision and qualifying final-boundary
evidence; it cannot inherit authorization from the abandoned plan or reinterpret
client-side mechanics as delivery. The planned fixture interface and raw-file
versus deployed-body treatment choices remain inapplicable while no evaluated
intervention is authorized.
