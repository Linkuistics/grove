# Runtime evidence — executable owner and historical source

Independent Ravel owns the compiled policy worker and its pinned Bun runtime.
Its [runtime evidence](https://github.com/Linkuistics/Ravel/blob/v0.1.0/docs/runtime-evidence.md),
[worker decision](https://github.com/Linkuistics/Ravel/blob/v0.1.0/docs/adr/policy-evaluation-precedes-the-launch.md)
and [executable specification](https://github.com/Linkuistics/Ravel/blob/v0.1.0/docs/specs/session-execution.md)
retain the applicable controls, source findings, observations and limits.

The full original experiments remain readable at the immutable
[published pre-extraction 22.1.0 revision](https://github.com/Linkuistics/grove/blob/825025bd8a644d6857475fd37ac64b511f6a37e5/docs/design/harness-selection-and-execution/runtime-evidence.md).
They describe the original executable and its archive runs, not acceptance of
independently released Ravel or Grove's current caller. Ravel's current evidence
links that provenance explicitly. The existing local anchors below keep inbound
historical citations usable without retaining executable implementation claims
as Grove's current contract.
